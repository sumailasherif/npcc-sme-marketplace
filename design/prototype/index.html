    Principles

    Four rules that keep the hub useful and honest.

    Category first

    If a customer taps Handicrafts, they see only handicrafts. No “related” noise, no paid placement.

    District first

    Location is a district before it is a coordinate. Geolocation is optional, never required.

    Money is computed

    Totals, profit and balance sheet come from SQL. AI writes the sentence, never the number.

    AI drafts, owners decide

    Listings, replies and invoices are drafts until the owner approves them.

    Iteration loop

    Two weeks per loop. Every loop ends with a real shop and a real customer.
